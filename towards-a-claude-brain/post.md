Organizing Knowledge
----------------
- So in order to in order to produce good outcomes from our Outcome Factories, we need:
    - WHAT
    - WHY
    - The knowledge necessary to make it happen
- Best stored in a tool: Todoist, Notion, Google Drive, Github
    - It's all knowledge -> knowledge base
<!-- TODO maybe make a whole post on how I organize my knowledge base -->
- Magical feature of knowledge bases is the linking:
    - With linking you can create "knowledge nodes" that reference other knowledge nodes
    - Gives you a single source of truth to update
    - Links can be _anything_ "look here, this Todoist ID, this page name, a URL"
- Knowledge bases are an external brain:
    - The paths through the base that you consume are like circuits in a brain
    - Each knowledge node is like a "function" that injects knowledge into Claude's context
    - Just like synapses, whether a link gets followed is probabilistic (and that's the power: you get signal without getting noise)
    - We can strengthen synapses by changing the language (e.g. "Maybe interesting: $URL" vs "YOU MUST READ: $URL")
- Outcomes are special, because they can be completed or not
    - How I organize my outcomes: Fractal Outcomes
